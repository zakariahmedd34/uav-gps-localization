**1\. Video Processing and Data Throughput**  
We are using a GoPro Hero 13 for aerial search missions. One challenge we are facing is the trade-off between video quality and processing time. Higher resolutions (e.g., 4K) and higher frame rates significantly increase the amount of data that must be processed during the mission.  
Our current tests using 720p video at one frame every two seconds produced accurate detections, but processing a 7-minute video still required approximately 15 minutes.  
How did your team handle video-processing bottlenecks and large data volumes? Did you use techniques such as frame sampling, resolution reduction, edge processing, distributed/big-data pipelines, or other optimizations to achieve near real-time performance?

Inorder to reduce the amount of data being processed, which did use techniques such as frame sampling and, edge computing via raspberry pi. It is to be noted that for our specific problem statement we were required to identify yellow spots on the ground and thus used HSV thresholding for the same rather than an ML model. Furthermore, due to the fact that these spots were separated at a considerable distance from each other and static, real time video processing would contain largely redundant visual information, making frame sampling the optimal choice to achieve near real-time performance without a large onboard computer.  
   
However, if real  time video processing is absolutely crucial, either an extremely good (but usually heavy) on board compute is required or a strong ground station with a high bandwidth and low latency communication link is required.
